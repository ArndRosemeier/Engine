# Sample assets

- `props.fmpk`: generated by AssetGenerator (Arnd Rosemeier).
- `character.glb`: a character exported from [Actors](https://github.com/ArndRosemeier/Actors)
  (`Character.exportGlb`, outfit "T-shirt and jeans", hair "short03", clips idle, walk,
  jog, sword attack, talk). Its parts are CC0 1.0: the MakeHuman base mesh, rig, photo
  skin, hair, eyes and garments (MakeHuman system assets and community asset packs) and
  the Quaternius Universal Animation Library clips. Details in Actors' NOTICE-ASSETS.md.
